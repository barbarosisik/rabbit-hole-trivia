RABBIT HOLE

MAC: Open Rabbit Hole.app from the Mac release ZIP.
WINDOWS: Extract the Windows ZIP, then open Play Rabbit Hole.cmd.
Windows background mode needs Node.js 24 or newer and Windows PowerShell 5.1.

Play now: ten questions, difficulty, chill/timed play, streaks and 50/50.
Play from background: three questions per turn, every 15 minutes by default.
Choose a category, answer each question, and click Next between questions.
After answer three, your turn score appears for eight seconds and fades away.

Use the Mac menu-bar arrow or Windows tray icon to pause or quit.
No questions, scores or settings are saved. No launch-at-login is installed.
See README.md for download and setup instructions.
