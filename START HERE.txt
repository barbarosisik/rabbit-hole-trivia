RABBIT HOLE - HOW TO PLAY

Double-click Play Rabbit Hole.cmd in this folder.

PLAY NOW
Opens the normal ten-question game in your browser.

PLAY FROM BACKGROUND
Choose an interval (15 minutes by default), then click Play from background.
The window closes. A small prompt appears near the taskbar after each interval.
Click it, choose a topic, answer the question, and let it fade away.

Want to try it immediately? Right-click the Rabbit Hole system-tray icon and
choose Question now. The icon may be under the ^ hidden-icons arrow.
Use that menu to pause reminders or quit the background game.

Internet required. No question dataset or scores are saved.
The game files live in the hidden Rabbit Hole folder beside this launcher.
